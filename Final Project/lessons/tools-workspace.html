Beginner Lesson

Tools & Workspace

What you actually need and what you don’t.

You don’t need a workbench or expensive tools to build a PC.

Basic Tools

The essentials
“Enough”

PC building is mostly tightening screws and plugging cables. You can complete an entire build with just one tool.

Simple takeaway: If you have a #2 Phillips screwdriver, you’re basically set.
Required
#2 Phillips screwdriver
Helpful
magnetic tip, small tray
Skip
power tools, torque drivers

Workspace Setup

Where to build
“Environment”

Your workspace matters more than your tools. A stable surface and good lighting make mistakes less likely.

Simple takeaway: A kitchen table beats building on the floor.
Surface
large, flat, stable table
Lighting
bright overhead or desk lamp
Avoid
carpeted floors, clutter

Static Electricity

What actually matters
“Overblown”

Static damage is real, but modern components are far more resilient than the internet makes it sound. Simple precautions go a long way.

Simple takeaway: Don’t shuffle on carpet, and touch grounded metal occasionally or wear a electro-static arm strap.
Do
touch case/PSU occasionally
Don’t
build on carpet in socks
Optional
anti-static wrist strap

Staying Organized

Small habits that help
“Sanity”

PC builds have lots of small screws and cables. Staying organized prevents lost parts and unnecessary stress.

Simple takeaway: Take your time and keep screws separated.
Screws
cups, trays, or bowls
Cables
don’t plug everything at once
Pacing
no rush, breaks are fine
